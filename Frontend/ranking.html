How does the ranking system work?

Once fully live, users can select what they think the appropriate ranking should be.

This website uses a ranking system that is used by Ski resorts and MTB Trails.

🟢

Green Circle

Least thrilling rides, best for those who may not like rides!

Green circle rides are typically kiddie rides or rides that may be slow, no inversions, and have little to no airtime

🟦

Blue Square

Moderate rides, These rides offer a bit more of an intense expereince compared to green circle rides

These are typicallty rides that have only 1 inversion and dont go very fast, and have some moderate airtime!

♦️

Black Diamond

Intense rides, These rides offer a step above the blue square rides.

These are typically rides with a mutliple inversions, go fast, and experience a lot of airtime!

♦️♦️

Double Black Diamond

Most Intense rides,These rides can seem like a big step from black diamonds!

These are typically rides with many inversions, go very fast, and can have a ton of airtime, especially ejector airtime
